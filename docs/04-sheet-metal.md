# Sheet metal

The trays and the base pan are cut and bent by SendCutSend from the Rev 4 outlines:
`greenhouse/sendcutsend/make_dxf_rev4.py` writes `tray_DOWN.dxf`, `tray_UP.dxf` (3 of each) and
`basepan_DOWN.dxf`, `basepan_UP.dxf` (1 of each), 5052-H32 0.063 in, 25 mm lips, 41 × 41 notches round
the posts, a 40 mm lap over the mid rails.

Fitting them is steps 16 to 20 of the frame booklet (`docs/03-frame-assembly.md`). The base pan DXFs
were regenerated for the 559 depth at 0.4.0: re-upload them before ordering.
