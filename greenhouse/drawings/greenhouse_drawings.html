Cameras

Two only: a fixed 160 degree overview in the top back-left corner that frames all three tiers for the daily health pass, and an Intel RealSense D405 on the arm flange for per-plant recognition, depth and grasp poses. The three per-tier cameras of Rev B are gone.

Reach check

The arm base sits on the lane centreline, 188 mm in front of each pot centre. The myCobot 280 working radius is 280 mm, so pot centres and the back pot edges (245 mm) are inside the envelope with about 35 mm to spare. Harvesting is done top-down with the Z axis setting the height.

Airflow

Intake is low on the Tier A side panel. Air rises through the lane, which acts as a chimney between tiers, and leaves through the two roof fans. Each tier also has an 80 mm fan stirring air across the canopy.

Assembly order

  • Build the two side frames (U + D) flat on the floor and square them.
  • Stand them up and join with the back L members, then the front L members.
  • Fit shelf rails R, then both X rails, and check that they are parallel within 0.5 mm.
  • Anchor to the wall, then fit panels, trays and the gantry.

Fit

The mast is OpenBuilds C-Beam (40 × 80 with V grooves), not plain 4080 T-slot: Xtreme Solid V wheels need the groove. Its 40 mm face runs across the lane so the 80 mm web stays stiff along X travel. A SendCutSend 6 mm plate cantilevers off the C-Beam XLarge gantry set and puts the arm base on the lane centreline. Order that plate only after measuring the delivered arm base.

Motion safety

Before any gantry move, the supervisor stows the arm inside the 174 mm lane. Four Honeywell reed contacts and an Eaton M22 E-stop drive an Omron G9SE-201 safety relay, which cuts the Jackpot3 motor supply and the arm's 12 V. The StepperOnline 23HS22-4004D-B280 holds 2.8 Nm with power off, so the carriage stays where it stopped.

Where the numbers come from

Shoulder height 131.22 mm above the mounting face, upper arm 110.4 mm, forearm 96 mm, wrist chain 63.4 + 75.05 + 45.6 mm: Elephant Robotics' published DH table for the myCobot 280. The 280 mm working radius is the sum the maker quotes, and the inner circle is the fold limit where the elbow collides with the shoulder.

D405 placement

The RealSense D405 sees from 70 mm to about 500 mm with an 87 by 58 degree field, so it images one pot at a time from the wrist. Keep the last 70 mm of approach on odometry alone: below the minimum range the depth image goes blank, which is the usual cause of a failed grasp.

Fit check

The tank is the constraint: 762 x 254 x 178 mm leaves 555 mm of width and 100 mm of front strip for everything else. The three LED drivers hang from the underside of the deck above the tank, shown dashed, which keeps their heat out of the bay floor and their AC away from the water line.

Verify before drilling

Footprints are datasheet nominals: LRS-350-24 215 x 115, HLG-60H 190 x 63, TL-SG108PE 158 x 101, Jetson carrier 103 x 90.5, Jackpot3 about 105 x 75, G9SE-201 22.5 wide on DIN. Measure the boxes you actually receive before you drill the mounting plate.

What runs where

  • ESPHome nodes: sensors, lights, fans, watering and every safety interlock. They keep running if Home Assistant is down.
  • Home Assistant: setpoints, zone watering decisions, alerts, dashboard.
  • Jetson: camera vision, robot supervisor, Jackpot3 (FluidNC) gantry control.

Network note

Every device here shares one subnet with the HA Yellow, so local control does not depend on the open pfSense routing issue. The Jetson needs that issue fixed for internet updates, and so does reaching its dashboard from VLAN 20.
